# Qualify a commercial lead

Inputs: source-supported claims, canonical entities, existing opportunity
history and a working stack definition. Use only these inputs for facts.

Return: proposed opportunity ID or existing match; stack and fit (direct,
enabling, adjacent); named Canadian beneficiary; buyer/investor/sponsor;
product, asset or work package; current stage; Canadian origin/site/work/IP/
income path; evidence gaps; next validation question; and update triggers.

Separate the documented baseline from your opportunity assessment. Label
inference explicitly. Missing buyer interest, scope, Canadian benefit or
availability keeps a record in the discovery queue. Do not fill gaps with a
plausible company name. A Canadian headquarters, Canadian market rights,
foreign subsidiary profit and Canadian exports are different attributes.

Match entities by legal name, aliases and identifiers, not branding alone.
Keep one parent transaction across multiple stacks and downstream reports.
Never approve a Top Ask. A government action must identify the decision,
authority, object, timing and responsible Canadian owner; otherwise return
no transaction-specific ask identified.
