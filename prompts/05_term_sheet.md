# Export a term sheet for human approval

Inputs: department-approved public/internal records in the appropriate
environment and explicit reviewer decisions. Do not ingest internal records
into a public student system.

Use at most two pages and exactly these three columns:
STRATEGIC STACK | COUNTRY CONTEXT & STRENGTHS |
AREAS OF CURRENT & FUTURE COOPERATION.
Within column three use TOP ASKS and OTHER OPPORTUNITIES.

TOP ASKS may contain only records with documented approval, named decision
authority, concrete object, timing and Canadian owner. Use direct action
language. Do not invent a deadline or request a decision already made. If
none qualifies say no transaction-specific ask validated. Candidate market
access actions may appear in a clearly labelled validation draft only.

OTHER OPPORTUNITIES should identify named deals/projects or explicitly
label an early lead. Use source IDs with a separate annex for evidence and
review notes. Do not put estimated gross project value forward as Canadian
benefit. No unapproved external publication or distribution.
